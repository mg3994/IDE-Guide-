# Google Flow Video & Shorts Prompt Engineering Guidelines

This document outlines the standard prompting framework for generating YouTube videos and Shorts for the **IDE Guide** channel (`https://www.youtube.com/@IDEguide`) using generative video pipelines such as **Google Flow** and AI video synthesizers.

---

## 1. Visual Aesthetics & Quality Benchmarks

To create visually engaging tech content that maximizes audience retention:

- **Resolution & Aspect Ratios**:
  - Long-form YouTube: `16:9` (1920x1080 or 3840x2160 UHD)
  - YouTube Shorts: `9:16` (1080x1920)
- **Lighting & Ambiance**:
  - Dramatic cinematic rim lighting (cyberpunk blues, emerald greens, warm ambers).
  - High-end developer workstation setup: matte black mechanical keyboards, ultra-wide curved monitors, acoustic panelling, diffused neon backlighting.
- **Camera Dynamics**:
  - Macro close-up tracking shots across glowing IDE code editors.
  - Smooth fluid camera motion (dolly-in, pan-tilt, orbit) with shallow depth-of-field (bokeh effect on background).
- **Motion & On-Screen Elements**:
  - Smooth typing animations (120 FPS text flow).
  - Multi-file diff highlights (green additions, red removals).
  - Floating UI glassmorphic cards summarizing pricing and specs.

---

## 2. Multi-Scene Script Blueprint for Flow

Each generated video specification is divided into structured visual-audio beats:

### Scene 1: The Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual Prompt**: High-energy macro zoom into an IDE running dynamic code or encountering a critical bottleneck. High contrast lighting.
- **Audio / Narration**: Direct, provocative question addressing common developer frustrations (e.g., "Is your editor slowing you down or stealing your money?").
- **On-Screen Text**: Bold typography with dynamic drop shadow.

### Scene 2: The Core Showcase & Standout Features (5-20s)
- **Visual Prompt**: Smooth cinematic pan across the editor UI in action. Live multi-file refactoring, AI autocompletion, or lightning fast terminal builds.
- **Audio / Narration**: Concrete breakdown of what makes this editor unique and powerful.

### Scene 3: The Reality Check (Pros vs. Cons) (20-35s)
- **Visual Prompt**: Split-screen or dual-tone lighting contrast showing the strengths alongside memory usage, indexing lag, or cloud privacy trade-offs.
- **Audio / Narration**: Candid, unbiased critique of potential drawbacks and real-world dealbreakers.

### Scene 4: Pricing Transparency & Target Verdict (35-50s)
- **Visual Prompt**: Clean glassmorphic info-card displaying Free vs. Paid tiers, exact monthly pricing, and license type.
- **Audio / Narration**: Definitive answer on whether developers should pay or stick with free alternatives.

### Scene 5: Outro, CTA & Independence Disclosure (50-60s)
- **Visual Prompt**: Outro card with YouTube subscribe button animation, channel branding `@IDEguide`, and independent publication watermark.
- **Audio / Narration**: Call to action to subscribe, check the full blog review on `ideguide.blogspot.com`, and non-sponsorship disclaimer.

---

## 3. Mandatory Non-Sponsorship Disclaimer

Every video script must conclude with or display:
> *"Disclaimer: IDE Guide is an independent technical publication. This content is not sponsored, endorsed, or affiliated with any software vendor or editor developer mentioned above. All reviews and assessments are based strictly on objective developer evaluation."*
