JetBrains IntelliJ IDEA Review: Is It Worth It in 2026?

The Capable and Ergonomic Java IDE

Overview & Target Audience

Category: Full-Featured Enterprise IDE

Developer: JetBrains

Best For: Enterprise Java, Kotlin, Spring Boot, and backend developers.

JetBrains IntelliJ IDEA has captured significant attention in the software engineering space. Designed to streamline workflow efficiency, it targets developers looking for reliability, high performance, and ergonomic coding experience.

🟢 The Good (Strengths)

  • Unmatched Java, Kotlin, and Spring static analysis
  • Refactoring suite is industry benchmark
  • Deep database and framework integrations

🔴 The Bad (Limitations)

  • Heavy memory footprint (2GB+ RAM minimum)
  • Complex settings and steep learning curve

Pricing & Plan Options

Pricing Model: Freemium / Paid Commercial

Free Tier: Community Edition is free & open source for JVM/Android.

Paid Options: Ultimate Edition starts at $16.90/month or $169/year.

Final Verdict

If you are looking for an editor tailored for high velocity, JetBrains IntelliJ IDEA is definitely worth evaluating. Check your team's workflow requirements and trial the free tier first.
